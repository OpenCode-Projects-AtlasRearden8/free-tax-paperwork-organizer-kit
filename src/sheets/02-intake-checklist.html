The unpacking list for a tax folder

A tax shoebox always weighs more than it looks. This is the runbook for emptying it: every form that might arrive between January and spring, with a box for have it and a box for still waiting. Waiting boxes are honest boxes — they tell you exactly who to nudge.

Form / paper In friendly words Have it Waiting n/a
Jobs and wages
W-2One from every employer you had, including brief jobs and side gigs with a payroll department.
Final pay stub of the yearThe bridge until the real W-2 shows up. Compare the two, then retire the stub.
Freelance and self-employed
1099-NECMoney clients paid you directly for work. One per client who paid enough to report.
1099-MISCOther payments: rent to you, prizes, awards, that sort of thing.
1099-KThe card-app and marketplace report: payment apps, storefronts, delivery platforms.
Banks and investments
1099-INTInterest a bank paid you, even modest amounts.
1099-DIVDividends and distributions from investments you hold.
1099-BStock or fund sales: each sale became a line on here.
Year-end statementsBank and card December statements. Your year in one place.
Home, health, and family
1098 (mortgage)The mortgage statement lenders mail in January.
Property tax bill or receiptThe annual bill, or the county’s printed year-end summary.
1098-T (tuition)College and coursework statements, for you or someone you support.
Childcare provider statementProvider name, tax number, amount paid. Ask them for it if none arrived.
Health-insurance letter (1095)Marketplace or employer coverage paperwork that health plans send out.
Medical & pharmacy summariesStatements from insurers and pharmacies; your own tally if you kept one.
Retirement, government, and the unusual
1099-R (retirement)Distributions from pensions, 401(k)s, IRAs.
SSA-1099 (benefits)Social Security benefits statement.
1099-G (government)State refunds, unemployment notices, similar government payments.
K-1 (partnerships)If an investment or family business sent you one, it tends to arrive fashionably late.

Warm tip

Keep this sheet on the folder’s front page. When a form arrives, tick have it and drop it behind this sheet — filing takes ten seconds and removes one open loop from your mind. If a sender is letting you down, tick waiting and write the sender’s name in the margin. Missing forms almost always mean a quick message to that sender’s HR, bank, or platform resolved everything.
