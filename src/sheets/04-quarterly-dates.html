The official deadlines can slide a day or two when they land on a weekend or holiday, and they differ by state and situation. The government’s own current-year calendar is the source of truth — these boxes are where your looked-up dates get kept, not where they get invented.

Repeat gently: how the four dials in a filing season

  • When the first date rolls around, your folders already hold the ground you gained from filing season — set your reminder dates in one sitting for the whole year.
  • Each done box earns its receipt. Clip the payment confirmation to this page — that’s what the confirmation column is for.
  • After the January date, Sheet 5’s give-away rules decide what leaves the year’s folders — the loop closes, and a new one starts.

Warm tip

Set a phone reminder about a week before each your date, titled “quarterly paperwork — check the folder.” Future-you is always a different, busier person; present-you doing ninety seconds of calendar typing is the kindest meeting of the two. And if a quarter gets missed, the folder and this page tell you plainly what was and wasn’t sent — which is exactly the information that makes the fix easy.
